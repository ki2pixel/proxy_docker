# Account Suspension on EarnFM: Possible Reasons and Resolution

If your EarnFM account has been suspended, there are various reasons this might have occurred. Here's a list of potential causes:

## Possible Reasons for Account Suspension

- **Multiple Accounts:** Creating and using more than one EarnFM account is against our terms.
- **VPN Usage:** Sharing your internet through a VPN, as we exclusively seek residential IPs.
- **Data Center IPs:** Connecting using an IP address from a data center.
- **Abnormal Activity:** Suspicious behavior or activity that violates our terms of service.
- **Misuse of Offers:** Exploiting offers, discounts, or coupon codes in unintended ways.
- **Violation of Terms:** Any other breach of our terms and conditions.

## Seeking Resolution

- Our automated systems enforce these rules to ensure fairness.
- If you believe your suspension is unjust or in error, please create a support ticket.
- Our team will review your case and provide further assistance.

EarnFM is committed to maintaining a fair and secure environment for all users.
