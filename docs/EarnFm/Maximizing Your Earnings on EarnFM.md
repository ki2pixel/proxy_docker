# Maximizing Your Earnings on EarnFM

Earnings depend on demand for your region and how reliably your devices stay connected.

## Ways to increase earnings

- Keep devices connected as continuously as possible.
- Run EarnFM on multiple devices, each on a **different IP / network**, under one account.
- Use the referral program to earn 10% of invitees' earnings.

### Wi-Fi requirement

EarnFM requires a **Wi-Fi / fixed broadband** connection for sharing. Mobile data (cellular) alone is **not supported** for earning.
