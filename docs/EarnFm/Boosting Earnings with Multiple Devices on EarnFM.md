## Boosting Earnings with Multiple Devices on EarnFM

**Maximize your earning potential on EarnFM by utilizing multiple devices. Here's how it works and what you need to know:**

## Increasing Earnings with More Devices

- **Earnings Multiplier:** Each additional device actively running EarnFM contributes to your total earnings.
- **Potential for Higher Earnings:** More devices mean a greater cumulative earning potential.

## Key Guidelines to Follow

1. **One Device Per Network:** Each device should be connected to a unique IP or network.
2. **Single Account Usage:** Operate all devices under one EarnFM account for consistency and adherence to our terms.

By following these guidelines, you can effectively increase your earnings on EarnFM.
