# Understanding "Foreground Only" Mode in EarnFM iOS App

For iOS users of EarnFM, it's important to understand the "Foreground only" mode and how it affects your earning potential:

## EarnFM iOS App: Foreground Only Mode

- **Active App Requirement:** The EarnFM app must be open and visible on your screen to earn.
- **iOS Background Limitation:** iOS policies restrict apps from running in the background.
- **Earning Process:** To earn, users must keep the EarnFM app actively open in the foreground.

This mode ensures compliance with iOS guidelines while allowing users to earn through the app.
