# Diverse Payout Methods on EarnFM

The minimum withdrawal amount is **\$15**.

EarnFM offers a range of payout methods to ensure flexibility and convenience for its users. Here are the payout options currently supported:

## PayPal

- **Global Accessibility:** PayPal is widely accepted worldwide, providing a convenient way to receive your earnings.

## Crypto

- **Cryptocurrency Options:** Choose from Bitcoin, Litecoin, and USDT TRC20 for cryptocurrency enthusiasts.
- **Versatile Use:** Cryptocurrencies can be sold, spent online, or used for various transactions.

## Gift Cards

- **Online Shopping:** Opt for gift cards from Amazon.com (US) and Starbucks (US).
- **Convenient Purchases:** Use gift cards for online shopping and enjoy your favorite beverages.

EarnFM is continuously working to expand its payout options, offering users even more choices in the near future.
