# Getting Started with EarnFM

**Embarking on your journey with EarnFM is straightforward and rewarding. Here's a step-by-step guide to begin earning passive income:**

## Steps to Start Using EarnFM

### Simple Registration:

1. Begin by registering on the EarnFM platform.

### Download the Application:

1. Download the EarnFM app suitable for your device [here](#).
2. Available for both mobile and desktop operating systems.

### Install and Log In:

1. After installation, log in to your EarnFM account.

### Start Sharing Bandwidth:

- Click the "connect" button to begin sharing your bandwidth.
- Stay connected to maximize earnings.

### Please Note

- **Earnings Update:** It may take up to a week for earnings to reflect in your account.
- **Best Practice:** For optimal results, maintain a continuous connection.

EarnFM makes it easy to earn passive income by sharing your bandwidth.
