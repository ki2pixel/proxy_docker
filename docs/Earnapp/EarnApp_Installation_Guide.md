# ? Can I install EarnApp on Hosting Services, Virtual Machines or Dockers?

**Ricardo Seixas**  
Updated 1 year ago

No. Installing EarnApp on Virtual Machines (VMs), Docker containers, or hosting services is strictly prohibited.
EarnApp is designed exclusively for use on personal, residential devices, such as your home computer, smartphone, or Smart TV, that are part of your daily life.

## Prohibited Environments:

*   Virtual Machines (VMs)
*   Docker containers
*   Cloud hosting services (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud)
*   Personal or home servers
*   Any device used for business or monetization purposes

---

## ▲ Important Notice:

If our system detects that EarnApp is running in a virtualized or unauthorized environment:

*   **Your account will be terminated without prior notice**
*   Any pending payments will be canceled
*   You will be in violation of EarnApp's Terms of Use

## ✅ Why Only Personal Devices?

EarnApp is meant to provide a fair and simple way to earn a bit of extra "pocket money" by sharing your unused internet bandwidth. It's ideal for covering small expenses like:

*   Discord Nitro
*   Netflix subscriptions
*   Occasional treats

It is not intended to be a primary source of income or used for commercial purposes.
